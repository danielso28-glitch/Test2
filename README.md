# Two Paths – prototype

Clickable prototype of a student project (Advanced Design Thinking, FH Münster):
people 50+ see themselves at 75 on two paths – without and with exercise – and join a local exercise group.

**Try it:** open the GitHub Pages link of this repo on your phone.

- All groups, insurers, stories and scores are **demo data**.
- Your account, face photo and progress are stored **only on your own phone** (browser storage). Nothing is sent to us.
- Please don't enter real insurance numbers – keep the example number.
